📋 How to Play

Goal: Collect all 32 cards.

How to play:

  1. 32 cards are shuffled and evenly distributed (16 each).
  2. You choose a category on your top card (Weight, Length, Lifespan, or Dive).
  3. The computer reveals its top card.
  4. Whoever has the higher value wins both cards.
  5. In case of a tie, cards go to the jackpot – the next winner gets everything.

Tip: Bigger is not always better – some whales are unbeatable in certain categories!
